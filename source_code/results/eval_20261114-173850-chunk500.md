### Q03 [program requirements] What are the requirements for a minor in Computer Science?

**Expected:** 24 credits / six courses: one programming course (CS101, CS103 or CS104), one 100/200-level CS course, CS201, one intermediate course, one 200/300-level CS course, one advanced course.

**Answer:** The requirements for a minor in Computer Science are as follows:

- Complete at least one Computer Science course (MATH 202 - Discrete Math is also counted).
- Take a total of six courses:
  - One programming course (4 credits): CS 101: Introduction to Programming, CS 103: Programming for Data Science and Visualization, or CS 104: Introduction to Computer Science.
  - One 100-level or 200-level CS course (4 credits).
  - CS 201 – Data Structures & Algorithms (4 credits).
  - One intermediate course (4 credits).
  - One 200-level or 300-level CS course (4 credits).

This totals 24 credits for the minor.
